## SLIDE 7: PRODUCT - THE ASSESSMENT STATION

### Headline
**"60 Seconds to Know Everything About a Member's Technique"**

### Talking Points

**The Core Product: AI Assessment Station**

1. **Hardware Setup (Minimal & Affordable)**
   - iPad Pro (or any tablet) mounted on adjustable stand
   - Positioning: 8-10 feet from member, chest height
   - Optional: LED ring light for consistent lighting
   - Total hardware cost: $800-1,200 per station
   - Gym installs once, works for years

2. **The Assessment Process**
   - Member performs standardized 60-second routine
   - Routine includes: stance, jab, cross, hook, uppercut, slip, roll, footwork
   - AI camera captures 30 fps video
   - MediaPipe extracts 33 body keypoints per frame = 1,800 data points
   - PyTorch model classifies technique quality across 12 dimensions

3. **Biomechanical Analysis (The Secret Sauce)**
   - **Stance Analysis**: Weight distribution, guard position, base width
   - **Punch Mechanics**: Hip rotation, shoulder alignment, follow-through
   - **Defensive Positioning**: Head movement quality, guard recovery
   - **Footwork Patterns**: Step efficiency, balance maintenance, pivot quality
   - **Power Generation**: Kinetic chain analysis (legs → hips → shoulders → fist)
   - **Injury Risk Flags**: Hyperextension, poor alignment, imbalance indicators

4. **Output: Actionable Insights for Coaches**
   - Color-coded skill matrix (red/yellow/green across 12 dimensions)
   - Top 3 priority areas for improvement
   - Recommended workout focus for next 4 weeks
   - Injury risk warnings
   - Historical comparison (for returning members)
   - Exports to member app + coach dashboard

### Key Statistics To Include

- **60 seconds** - Complete assessment time
- **33 keypoints** - Body tracking markers (MediaPipe)
- **1,800+ data points** - Per assessment (33 points × 30fps × 2 sec per move)
- **12 dimensions** - Technique quality metrics
- **95% accuracy** - Technique classification vs expert coaches
- **$1,000** - Hardware cost per station (vs $50K+ for traditional motion capture)

**For Founder When Pitching:**

1. **Tell a Story**: Start with a member's experience (before/after Gideon)
2. **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Have demo video ready (60-sec assessment in action)
3. **Know Your Numbers Cold**: ARR, CAC, LTV, churn - memorize them
4. **Address Concerns Proactively**: "You might be thinking... here's why that's not an issue"
5. **End with Urgency**: "We're closing this round in 4 weeks, several investors interested, would love to have you as part of founding team"

- Slides 1-2 (Problem): 2 minutes - paint the pain
- Slides 3-4 (Solution): 3 minutes - show the vision
- Slides 5-6 (Market): 2 minutes - prove the opportunity
- Slides 7-8 (Product): 2 minutes - demo if possible
- Slide 9 (Business Model): 1 minute - show you understand business
- Slides 10-13 (Traction/Competition/GTM/Financials): 3 minutes - prove you can execute
